### F1 [stage-1] A drift step of 10–10.65 ms is never re-aligned in one step

`src/DualPlayer.Core/Sync/OutputSync.cs:37`, `:102`; AC-07 / spec §6 row "Recovery after a drift step". The new row covers any step "by more than the tolerance at once", but the step path counts only readings beyond `RealignThreshold` (tolerance + 0.5 ms). The reviewer simulated the controller's constants: a 10.3 ms step takes 0.8 s to return within 10 ms, 10.5 ms takes 1.2 s, 10.6 ms takes 1.4 s; only steps of about 10.7 ms or more are re-aligned at 0.3 s. Impact is under 1 ms beyond the tolerance, but the wording and the code disagree and no test covers the band. Suggested: count step readings against the tolerance, or reword the row to "beyond the re-alignment threshold (tolerance + 0.5 ms)".

Verdict: Fix now (owner chose to reword the spec, not the code) — the §6 row now names the re-alignment threshold, tolerance + 0.5 ms (`ad3205d`); `OutputSyncTests` pins that a 10.4 ms error is left to the rate correction (`607821b`).

`test-plan.md:50-52`, `:97-106`; `sad.md:18`, `:516-519`; AC-07. The step-recovery row has no test-plan row and no NFR-validation scenario; QG-1 still lists only the 1 s recoveries and does not name the guaranteed conditions.

### F3 [stage-1] The pinning test checks when the re-alignment was requested, not that the output is back within tolerance

`tests/DualPlayer.Core.Tests/Playback/AudioEngineSyncRecordTests.cs:388`; AC-07 / §6 step-recovery row. Nothing asserts a drift reading at or before 0.5 s after the step. Suggested: assert that the stepped device's `drift-trace` readings between 4.6 s and 5.0 s are all within 10 ms.

Verdict: Fix now — the test now finds the step in the `drift-trace` readings and asserts exactly three readings beyond the tolerance and every reading from 0.5 s after the step within 10 ms (`607821b`).

### F4 [stage-2] Most steps now leave no excursion and no drift-trace

`src/DualPlayer.Core/Playback/AudioEngine.cs:937`, `:955-968`; AC-07 (observability). A step removed after three readings averages to about 3/10 of its size over the 1 s record, so steps up to about 33 ms open no excursion and flush no trace: in the target-PC run 45 steps were re-aligned and 9 appear in the trace. It matches the records-based definition in §6, so it is not a compliance failure, but the diagnostics of the first fix now miss most steps. Suggested: let a step re-alignment trigger the trace flush, and/or say in the spec that a step removed within 0.5 s counts as a correction and may not show as an excursion.

Verdict: Fix now — every step re-alignment flushes the trace (`607821b`, with a 20 ms step test that has no excursion); the §6 row says a step removed this fast may open no excursion (`ad3205d`).

### F5 [stage-2] The new decision logic has no tests for its failure paths, and the fallback path is untested

`src/DualPlayer.Core/Sync/OutputSync.cs:67-75`, `:100-105`; `src/DualPlayer.Core/Playback/AudioEngine.cs:898-912`, `:929-935`; AC-07, AC-13. No `OutputSyncTests`: nothing checks that one or two readings do not re-align, that alternating sides reset the count, that `Reset()` and a re-alignment clear it, or that a recovery window or a pending re-alignment blocks the step path. The once-per-second fallback lost its only test.

Verdict: Fix now — `tests/DualPlayer.Core.Tests/Sync/OutputSyncTests.cs`, nine tests incl. the once-per-second fallback (`607821b`). The recovery-window and pending-re-alignment blocks are covered at this level through `mayRealign: false`, not by a separate engine test.

### F6 [stage-2] The 4.6 s assertion depends on how fast the control loop runs

`tests/DualPlayer.Core.Tests/Playback/AudioEngineSyncRecordTests.cs:377-388`. The sync sample reads fake time when the control loop actually runs, while the test keeps advancing it; on a loaded runner the margin of 200 ms of fake time (about 20 render steps) can be missed. Suggested: stop advancing until the re-alignment line appears, or take the timing from the trace readings.

Verdict: Fix now — the step tests advance with `RenderSettled`, which waits for the control loop after every 10 ms step, and take the timing from the trace readings (`607821b`).

## Checked and found fine

- No double re-alignment: after a step re-alignment the latest drift is 0, both paths wait for `RealignsApplied >= RealignsRequested`, and readings are blocked until the render thread applies the request.
- The step path never runs while an alignment is pending; `Sync.Reset()` clears the counter on resume, seek and when a stalled output comes back; lost and paused outputs are not sampled.
- Sign handling matches the existing path; no oscillation in the injected-step tests.
- AC-13: a step re-alignment counts one correction; start, seek and resume alignment steps only log; a steady ±ppm run still counts 0.
- No existing test was weakened; the one edit (`AverageDriftMs` → `Readings`) follows the intended behaviour change.
- Module boundaries and conventions hold: `DriftTrace` is in `Core/Diagnostics`, source-generated `LoggerMessage`, no `DateTime` / `Stopwatch`, everything new runs on the engine-control loop.
- The `DriftTrace` ring logs each reading once, oldest first; worst-case log volume stays within the rolling cap.
- Build 0 warnings; `DualPlayer.Core.Tests` 244 / 244 pass; CONTEXT.md, cli.md and sad.md flow 7 match the new behaviour.
